Haines hosts one of the world's great bald eagle gatherings. Guided viewing tours access the Chilkat Bald Eagle Preserve and river corridors where naturalists know active perches, feeding patterns and photography angles.

  • ✓Small groups (often 14 or fewer) with expert naturalist guides

  • ✓Summer nest sites and fishing eagles; winter peak concentrations Oct–Feb
  • ✓Combine with Chilkat Preserve context for deeper interpretation

Frequently Asked Questions

How many eagles will I see?

Summer calls typically yield multiple sightings; winter peaks can reach thousands along the Chilkat.

Is this suitable for children?

Yes — easy viewing from vehicles and short walks; bring layers for cool weather.

Do I need my own binoculars?

Helpful but most tours provide scopes and spare binoculars.
