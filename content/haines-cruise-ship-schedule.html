Major Alaska cruise lines call at Haines on select Inside Passage itineraries — often paired with Skagway, Juneau and Glacier Bay. Schedules vary by year; always confirm with your cruise line.

Typical pattern: ships arrive morning to midday and depart late afternoon, giving 6–10 hours ashore. Haines is a dock port — no tender required.
