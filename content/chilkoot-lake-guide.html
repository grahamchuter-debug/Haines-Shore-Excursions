Chilkoot Lake State Park combines glacier-fed water, rainforest mountains and reliable wildlife viewing — bears fishing for salmon, bald eagles overhead and seals in the lake. It is the signature Haines shore excursion for cruise passengers who want pristine Alaska without Skagway-scale crowds.

Most tours run 2.5–4 hours with naturalist guides in groups of 14 or fewer. Pier pickup is standard; the drive from downtown Haines takes roughly 30 minutes.
