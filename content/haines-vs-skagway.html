Both Haines and Skagway sit on the Inside Passage, but they offer fundamentally different cruise port experiences. This guide helps you choose — or appreciate what makes each stop unique on an Alaska itinerary.

Which port is better for you?

Choose Haines if bald eagles, bear viewing, Chilkoot Lake scenery and a genuine Alaska town matter more than railway nostalgia. Haines is Skagway's quieter, more wilderness-focused neighbour.

Choose Skagway if the White Pass & Yukon Route railway, Gold Rush walking tours and high-energy historic excursions are your priority.

Many Alaska itineraries visit both ports on different days — use Haines for wildlife and Skagway for railway history.

Why cruise passengers choose this

  • Best for cruise passengers: Wildlife-first travellers who want fewer crowds
  • Wildlife opportunities: Haines: eagles, bears, marine life; Skagway: mountain goats, historic sites
  • Return-to-ship timing: Both ports suit standard 6–10 hour calls with local operator buffers
