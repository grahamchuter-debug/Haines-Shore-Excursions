Where Ships Dock

The main pier sits beside Portage Cove in downtown Haines. No tender required — you walk straight into a walkable, low-key port town.

Getting To Tours

Wildlife tours to Chilkoot Lake and the Chilkat Valley typically include pier pickup. Allow 15–30 minutes drive to lake and river trailheads.

Currency

US dollars. Cards accepted; carry cash for small vendors.

Weather

Layer up — southeast Alaska is cool and changeable even in summer. Rain gear helps.

Wildlife Season

Salmon runs peak late summer–fall for bears and eagles. Eagles concentrate Oct–Feb at Chilkat.
