Frequently Asked Questions

When are bears most active?

During salmon runs, roughly July through September at the lake outlet.

What is the drive time?

About 30 minutes from downtown Haines to the lake.

Express or full tour?

Express fits tighter schedules; full tour adds lunch and more viewing time.
