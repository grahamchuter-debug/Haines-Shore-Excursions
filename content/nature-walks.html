Frequently Asked Questions

What is forest walk therapy?

A guided mindful walking practice combining meditation, sensory awareness and nature — originated in Japan as shinrin-yoku.

Is this physically demanding?

No — gentle paths suitable for most mobility levels.

Good for a second port day?

Excellent if you have already done Chilkoot Lake on a previous Alaska itinerary.
